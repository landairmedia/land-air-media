Real estate photography FAQs

Answers before
you book.

What is included in your real estate photography packages?+

Packages can include MLS-ready property photography, floor plans, aerial coverage when applicable, video, virtual staging, and delivery options for web, social, email, and print. The final mix depends on the property, marketing goal, and selected package.

How quickly will I receive my real estate photos?+

We confirm the expected turnaround with the scope and schedule before the shoot. We typically respond to new inquiries within one business day, and rush delivery can be discussed when availability allows.

How should I prepare a home for real estate photography?+

Declutter the main rooms, complete basic cleaning, turn on or replace practical lights, open important window views, secure pets, and finish repairs that would distract from the listing. We can help identify the highest-impact preparation steps.

How much does real estate photography cost in Greater Atlanta?+

The pricing guide provides benchmarks by finished square footage, starting at $250. Many homes have specific needs, so we often build a custom package. We confirm the scope and any additional pricing with you before the shoot.

Is drone photography included with a listing shoot?+

Drone coverage depends on weather, airspace and FAA limitations, property access, and site conditions on the day. We confirm practical constraints before the shoot; if flying is not safe or permitted, drone images may be unavailable.

Do you offer virtual staging for vacant rooms?+

Yes. Virtual staging combines AI tools with hand finishing to create realistic, precisely scoped options for vacant rooms and alternate layouts. Staged images are clearly identified and do not replace accurate property photography.

What areas do you serve for real estate photography?+

Residential listing work serves Greater Atlanta, including Duluth and Gwinnett County. Travel fees may apply outside the core service area; share the property address so we can confirm availability and scope.

What happens if the weather or property is not ready?+

We review weather, access, occupancy, repairs, and listing timing before the shoot. If conditions would materially reduce the result or create an unsafe flight, we will recommend a practical reschedule or adjusted plan.

Can agents receive volume pricing or create a custom package?+

Yes. Volume and frequency discounts are available for agents, teams, and brokerages with recurring work. Packages can also be adjusted around the listing, budget, marketing channels, and preferred workflow.

How do I book a real estate photography shoot?+

Use the inquiry form or email directly with the property address, approximate size, occupancy, launch date, marketing goal, and desired deliverables. We respond with availability, questions, and a sensible next step.
