The person behind the camera

Understand the work.
Then show it well.

Before Land & Air Media, I spent ten years teaching high school physics and robotics. I left teaching to build a more flexible schedule for my family, support my wife’s photography business, and take on our commercial photography and video work.

I have always enjoyed making things across formats, from music production and graphic design to video editing and photography. That broad background lets me bring more than camera operation to a job: I can think through the marketing problem, give useful feedback, and solve production hurdles as they arise.

Over the past two years, I have worked with more than a dozen realtors, small businesses, and FCL Construction, including a year of monthly development photography. I hold a current FAA Part 107 certification and carry per-job insurance for aerial work.

I care less about media as an art object than about efficiency and problem solving. I enjoy streamlining recurring work, improving the performance of media assets, and building a process clients can rely on job after job.
