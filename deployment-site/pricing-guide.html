Editing, preparation & delivery

Every booking includes up to 20 minutes of on-site preparation. This can include replacing accessible light bulbs, tidying small areas of clutter, moving minor distractions, light branch trimming where appropriate, and similar quick adjustments. More involved preparation is $25 per additional 15 minutes. Significant digital clean-up is $30 per image. We’ll identify any exceptional needs and agree on the exact work and cost before shooting. AI-assisted retouching covers routine color, light, and cleanup while keeping the property accurate; Full and Premium use high-definition human editing. Damage, permanent features, and material facts are never concealed.
